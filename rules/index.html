Server Rules ⚔️

1️⃣ No griefing

Do not destroy or modify other players’ builds without permission.

2️⃣ No cheating or hacking

Hacks, cheats, X-ray, dupes, or unfair mods are strictly forbidden. If you ask an admin for the [HACKER] role, you can ignore this rule!

3️⃣ Be respectful in chat

No insults, bullying, or toxic behavior.

4️⃣ No stealing

Do not take items from other players’ chests or bases... unless you're 1000 blocks from spawn xd.

5️⃣ No exploiting bugs

If you find a bug, report it to staff instead of abusing it.

6️⃣ Keep builds appropriate

No offensive, inappropriate, or hateful builds.

7️⃣ KILL EVERYONE!!!!

Yea, it's the best idea in the world 😉.

8️⃣ Reduce lag

Avoid excessive redstone clocks, farms, or entities that cause lag.

9️⃣ Listen to staff

Admins and moderators can take action to keep the server fair and fun.

🔹 Unstable World

In "Unstable World" you can hack, grief, or do whatever you want. No rules apply here – go wild!
