The Studio Journey

2024
Created a website on a HP computer with Windows Server 2008 R2 and IIS7. We were called 'Adko5558 Services' with some cool projects like Adkoogle or Adktube.
2025
Created AdkVM under Adko5558 Services. Our website has moved from IP address to adkvm.onthewifi.com. AdkVM was a virtual machine service for free with high specs and many benefits. We also created a Minecraft Server called 'ZX Spectrum! Minecraft Server' Our player count record was 12 at once (without alts or bots).
Jan - Mar 2026
Many things have changed. Adko5558 Services has been renamed to 'ZX-Spectrum.online Dev Studio' and then 'ZX-Spectrum.online' (no more renames planned) and Minecraft Server went off because during the migration to our new rack server half of files were deleted because of my mistake including the world itself and all plugins and configuration. Most of the world has been recovered but it was extremely buggy and also there was not many players playing so we decided to shut it down for good. Server is still existing, but not running. We got a new server since March, it is HP ProLiant DL380 G7 with 60 GB of RAM and some storage.
Mar 2026 - now
We made this beautiful looking hub homepage, rebranded old YouTube channel, and started doing some other stuff like making Google Play apps, making good music on Spotify or make Minecraft Mods on Modrinth.

...and we keep going!
