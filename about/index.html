About ZX Spectrum! 🎮

ZX Spectrum! Minecraft Server was founded with the goal of creating a clean, stable, and engaging survival experience for players around the world 🌍. Since its inception, the server has supported a wide range of Minecraft versions, from 1.8.9 up to the latest 1.21.11, ensuring maximum compatibility for both old-school and modern clients. Players can enjoy pure survival gameplay with PvP everywhere, building, exploring, and competing without unnecessary distractions.

Over the years, ZX Spectrum! has evolved carefully. Our infrastructure is entirely self-hosted in Bratislava, Slovakia 🇸🇰 on a dedicated HP ProLiant DL380 G7 with 60GB of RAM, of which 17 GiB is allocated to Minecraft. With a blazing fast 4Gb/s connection, the server offers smooth performance and extremely low latency for players worldwide.

The admin team is professional and accessible. They have a dedicated staff room located right at spawn where they monitor gameplay, ensure fair play, and maintain server stability. Official admins are available to help players with questions, resolve conflicts, and manage PvP disputes in a fair and transparent manner.

We also maintain a collection of lightweight plugins designed to improve player convenience without affecting the core survival gameplay. This includes essential features such as /home, authentication with AuthMe, and a few backend management tools to ensure security and server stability.

Community is at the heart of ZX Spectrum!. While we currently host events as our player base grows, we plan to organize community competitions, Ice Boat Racing tournaments 🧊, and other collaborative or competitive activities. Players are encouraged to join our Discord server 💬 to interact with staff, give feedback, and be part of the evolving world of ZX Spectrum!.

Server Features ✨

🌍 Pure Survival

Focus on building, exploring, and PvP combat in a consistent and fair survival environment.

⚔ PvP Everywhere

Combat is enabled across the world, including spawn. Players always need to be alert and strategic.

🖥 Self-Hosted Performance

European-based dedicated server with high RAM and fast connection ensuring smooth gameplay.

🧊 Ice Boat Racing

Fast-paced, skill-based racing tracks for competitive players looking for extra challenges.

👥 Admin & Staff Team

Official admins work from a dedicated staff room at spawn, monitoring the server, helping players, and maintaining fair play.

At ZX Spectrum!, we prioritize player experience, stability, and community. Every design choice, from server hardware to gameplay mechanics, is made to create a world that players can trust, enjoy, and return to again and again. ⚡
